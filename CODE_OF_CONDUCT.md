# Code of Conduct

This project should be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Please do**
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or programming.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and help others get unstuck.

**Not okay**
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sharing someone else's private information.
- Spam or deliberately derailing a discussion.

**If something goes wrong**, contact the maintainer privately through a [private security report](https://github.com/frazz1234/claude-code-inspector/security/advisories/new) (it works for conduct issues too) or the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.
